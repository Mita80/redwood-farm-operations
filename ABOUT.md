About Redwood Farm Operations
Redwood Farm Operations is a digital farm‑management application designed to streamline livestock workflows, improve record accuracy, and support data‑driven decision‑making at Redwood Farms. Built using AppSheet and backed by secure cloud storage, the app centralizes all core operational processes into one easy‑to‑use platform.

🌿 What the App Does
The application provides structured tools for managing:

Pig profiles and herd information

Feeding logs and consumption tracking

Medication and treatment records

Breeding cycles and reproductive history

Mortality logs and health trends

Daily operational tasks

Farm dashboards and summaries

It is designed for both field and office use, ensuring staff can capture accurate data anywhere on the farm.

🐖 Who the App Is For
Redwood Farm Operations is intended for:

Farm managers

Livestock supervisors

Veterinary and health staff

Feeding and breeding teams

Administrative personnel

Role‑based access ensures each user sees only the information relevant to their responsibilities.

🌱 Why We Built It
Redwood Farms is committed to modernizing agricultural operations through:

Better data accuracy

Faster reporting

Improved animal welfare

Stronger operational oversight

Reduced paperwork

Real‑time visibility into farm performance

This app is the first module in a larger digital transformation initiative led by EzyGo Innovations Limited.
