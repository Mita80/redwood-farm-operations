PRIVACY POLICY (Public – End Users)
Effective Date: 29 September 2026 
Owner: Redwood Farms, under parent company EzyGo Innovations Limited
Application: Redwood Livestock Manager (Farm Operations App)
1. Introduction
Redwood Farms (“we”, “our”, “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the Redwood Livestock Manager application (“the App”).
By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.
2. Information We Collect
We collect only the information necessary to operate farm management features. This may include:
2.1 User Information
•	Name
•	Email address
•	Role or staff category
•	Activity logs related to app usage
2.2 Farm Operations Data
•	Livestock profiles (e.g., tag numbers, breed, age, health status)
•	Feeding logs
•	Medication and treatment records
•	Breeding records
•	Mortality records
•	Crop or vegetable operations (if enabled)
•	HR and staff attendance (if enabled)
•	Accounting entries (if enabled)
2.3 Device Information
•	Device type
•	Browser type
•	Basic diagnostic data (for performance and error reporting)
2.4 Automatically Collected Data
The App may collect anonymized usage analytics to improve performance and reliability.
3. How We Use Your Information
We use collected information to:
•	Operate and maintain the App
•	Manage livestock, crops, HR, and accounting workflows
•	Generate dashboards, summaries, and reports
•	Improve app performance and user experience
•	Provide customer support
•	Ensure data accuracy and operational efficiency
•	Maintain security and prevent misuse
We do not sell, rent, or trade your information.
4. Data Storage & Security
We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:
•	Secure cloud storage
•	Access controls and permissions
•	Encryption where applicable
•	Regular backups
•	Activity monitoring
Despite these measures, no system is completely secure. Users should follow best practices when entering or sharing data.
5. Data Sharing
We may share data only under the following conditions:
5.1 With Authorized Farm Staff
Users with permission may access operational data relevant to their role.
5.2 With Service Providers
If the App integrates with third party services (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics), those providers may process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements.
5.3 Legal Requirements
We may disclose data if required by law, regulation, or court order.
We never share data for advertising or marketing purposes.
6. User Rights
You have the right to:
•	Access your data
•	Request corrections
•	Request deletion (where applicable)
•	Request export of your data
•	Withdraw consent for optional data collection
To exercise these rights, contact us at: info@ezygoinnovations.com
7. Data Retention
We retain data only as long as necessary for operational, legal, or security purposes. Users may request deletion of specific records unless they are required for compliance or farm operational history.
8. Children’s Privacy
This App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
9. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the “Effective Date” will be updated. Continued use of the App indicates acceptance of the revised policy.
10. Contact Information
For questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, contact:
EzyGo Innovations Limited Chingola, Copperbelt Province, Zambia Email: info@ezygoinnovations.com
